- Developed with the ZIM.js canvas framework, with animated bubbles, sprite-sheet pop effects, and randomized audio.
- Implemented a dynamic difficulty system that ramps up spawn rate and bubble speed at timed intervals while increasing a score multiplier to reward survival.
- Built game state management for lives, score, and elapsed time, including a game-over summary screen and input locking to prevent post-game interactions.
